# Test report: #136, zones, settings and the kill switch in the console (v0.9.0 part D)

Date: 2026-11-11. Local Postgres only. No migration.

## What was built
- `listZones` with each zone's kill switch (`offer_enabled` is the zone being active).
- `getGlobalSettings` and `getZoneSettings`: every setting with its own value, the effective value, limits and versions, read in one snapshot.
- `requestGlobalSettingsChange` and `requestZoneSettingsChange`: checked at once against the limits, base_version and every zone (a trial run of `geo.ChangeSettings` in a savepoint, then undone); a change to what is already so is `no_change`. Approved by a second admin; at approval the version is checked again and the result must still be valid in every zone, or the approval fails (`stale_version`, `invalid_setting`) and nothing changes.
- `requestKillSwitch`: pauses or resumes a zone through an approval. Turning one on needs an area that overlaps no other active zone, checked at the request and again on approval.
