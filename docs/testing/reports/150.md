## What each part of the reply is
- **riders**: riders with an open shift (`ended_at IS NULL`) whose scooter is in the zone. Position is the latest `location_pings` row by `received_at`, with `last_ping_at`. A rider with no ping is shown at their shift's node with `last_ping_at` null (the contract needs lat and lng). Name is `riders.name`; no phone.
- **State, and which wins when several are true**: `on_order` over `offered` over `moving` over `on_break` over `idle`.
  - on_order: an unreleased offer in state `accepted` (the order is RIDER_ACCEPTED, PACKED, TIMER_RUNNING or ARRIVED; the offer is released at HANDED_OVER).
  - offered: an unreleased offer in state `open` that has not expired (an expired one waiting for the sweep is not an offer).
  - moving: a relocation in state `accepted` (a `suggested` one is not).
  - on_break: `shifts.break_started_at` is set.
  - An order and an offer cannot both hold (a rider has one live offer). The app refuses a break with an order or an offer, so the other overlaps are rare; the order is documented in `riderState`.
- **scooters**: scooters of the zone with status `active`: `code` as `plate`, `node_id`, `current_rider_id` as `rider_id`, and `stock_units` = sum of `scooter_stock.on_hand` (reserved units included).
- **orders**: paid and not yet handed over: PAYMENT_SECURED, OFFERED, NO_SCOOTER, RIDER_ACCEPTED, PACKED, TIMER_RUNNING, ARRIVED. `order_number` from `orders.OrderNumber`, the position of the gate version the order was placed to (`gate_history`), `rider_id` and `timer_started_at` (both null before they exist).
- **incidents_open**: incidents with status `open` or `assigned`. An incident has no zone column, so it takes its order's zone, else its rider's; one with neither is in no zone.
- **Rate limit**: the contract says polling faster than every 5 s gets 429 `rate_limited` with Retry-After. Kept in memory per (user, zone); a refused poll does not restart the wait.
- **Unknown or malformed zone id**: 404 `not_found`, as `orderTimeline` does for a bad id. Zones are not scoped by role for map.read (as `listZones`), and a hub_manager has no `map.read`.

## Privacy
Selected columns only: gate lat and lng; rider id and name; no customer, phone, gate label, entry notes or handover code is ever read. A test checks the exact field set of each object and that the raw body holds none of those strings.

## Tests added (`internal/console/live_test.go`, 12)
- Each state and the precedence: idle, offered, on_order, moving, on_break; an expired open offer, a suggested relocation and a released offer stay idle; order+move+break is on_order, offer+move+break is offered, move+break is moving. Reply validated against `LiveZone` of the built contract.
- Latest ping chosen (inserted newest first, so only `received_at` tells), another rider's ping ignored, a rider with no ping at their node with null `last_ping_at`.
- Other zones' riders, scooters and orders left out; an ended shift left out; the seven in-progress states shown and the seven others (PAYMENT_PENDING, PAYMENT_FAILED, HANDED_OVER, COMPLETED, CANCELLED, TIMED_OUT, FAILED) left out; zone B sees only its own.
- Scooter details: plate, node, rider, stock (two lots on one scooter summed), parked scooter without rider, maintenance scooter left out; order number shape, gate position, rider and timer start (and nulls before them).
- Incidents: open and assigned counted, closed not; zone by order wins over zone by rider; zone B's and the no-zone one are not counted.
- Empty zone fits the contract; permission (hub_manager 403 naming `map.read`, no token 401, support and admin 200); unknown and malformed ids 404; polling faster than 5 s is 429 with `Retry-After: 3` after 2 s, another zone is not limited, and after 5 s it answers again; privacy as above.
- Cost: `LiveZone` sends exactly 4 statements with an empty zone and with 12 riders and 12 orders; `EXPLAIN` (sequential scans off) names `orders_live_zone_idx` for the orders and `location_pings_rider_idx` for the latest ping.

## Deliberate breaks (20 compiled and ran, each reverted, all caught)
1 a move outranks an order; 2 a suggested relocation counts as moving; 3 an expired offer still counts as offered; 4 the oldest ping is chosen; 5 ended shifts included; 6 orders of every zone; 7 COMPLETED orders included; 8 closed incidents counted; 9 the rider's zone beats the order's for an incident; 10 a `phone` field on riders; 11 no rate limit; 12 no node fallback for a rider with no ping; 13 maintenance scooters listed; 14 an unknown zone is a 500; 15 the wrong permission; 16 ARRIVED left out of the in-progress states; 17 the break flag ignored; 18 stock takes the largest lot instead of the sum; 19 one extra statement per call; 20 a `label` on the gate. (A first pass of the break script ran twice at once and left one break in the file; I found it, restored the file, and re-ran breaks 3 and 19 clean. Break 1 first tried an offer over an order, which cannot be told apart in data because a rider has one live offer; it was replaced.)
