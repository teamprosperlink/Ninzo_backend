# Test report: the test payment stays on staging next to Razorpay (#263)

## Why
The customer app pays on staging with `POST /test/payments/{order_id}`. When Razorpay went on (#237), customer-api stopped mounting that route (404), so the app could not pay until it adds Razorpay Checkout. Owner, 2026-11-14: keep both on staging.

## What changed
- customer-api mounts the test-payment route on every environment except production, now also when Razorpay keys are set. Production never mounts it.
- A payment settled through the route becomes `provider = 'test'`. Its refunds are processed at once, with no money, and are never sent to Razorpay. Before this change such a refund would have stayed pending for ever, because Razorpay never saw the payment.

## Tests added
`TestTheTestPaymentNextToRazorpayMakesATestPayment`: an order made with a Razorpay-named provider and paid through the test route becomes a test payment, reaches PAYMENT_SECURED, and a cancel inside the window gives a processed refund.

## Deliberate break (1, reverted, caught)
The provider update made to match nothing: the payment stays `razorpay`, and the refund is not processed.
