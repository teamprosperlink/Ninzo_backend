# Test report: #265 part A, customers rate a delivery (contract and mock)

Date: 2026-11-14. Contract and mock only. The server side is part B. No migration.

## What was added
- Customer: `rateOrder` (POST `/orders/{order_id}/rating`, body `stars` 1 to 5 and optional `reason` from `late, cold_food, rude, wrong_items, other`; no free text; Idempotency-Key). Reply is the order (200). 404 for another customer's order, 400 for a bad body, 409 with the codes `not_delivered`, `already_rated`, `rating_window_closed`. The window is the console setting `rating_window_s`, named in the contract text and added by the server in part B.
- Customer Order: `rating` ({stars, reason, rated_at} or null) and `can_rate` (boolean), both required.
- Rider: `rating` ({average one decimal or null, count}) on the Rider profile (getRider, updateRider), optional until part B. Never a customer or an order.
- Console: `listRatings` (GET `/admin/ratings`, `orders.read`, filters zone_id, rider_id, stars, from, to, cursor and limit; rows hold order id and number, zone, rider, stars, reason, rated_at, never a customer detail). Timeline source enum gains `rating` (kind `rating.given`). RiderReview gains optional `rating` ({average, count}).
- Mock: all of these, with scenarios `already_rated`, `rating_window_closed` (customer), `rider_unrated` (rider), `ratings_empty` (console).

## Server replies that must change in part B
- Changed now, as a stand-in: `internal/orders/orders.go` sends `rating` null and `can_rate` false on the customer order (the contract requires both). Part B reads `order_ratings` and the window.
- Left out until part B (optional in the contract): the rider profile `rating` (getRider, updateRider) and the console rider review `rating`.
- Part B builds: `rateOrder`, `listRatings`, the timeline source `rating`, the `rating_window_s` zone setting, and the `order_ratings` table.

## Field lists that grew (`tests/contracts/rules_test.go`)
- `customerOrderFields`: added `can_rate` and `rating`. `riderOrderFields` did not change (the rider's rating is on the profile, not the order).
