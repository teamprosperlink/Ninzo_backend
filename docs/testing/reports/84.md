# Test report: offers (#84)

- Date: 2026-11-09

## Scope
v0.5.0 part 1. Offers to one rider at a time, once the 5-second cancel window closes: `getRiderState`, `listOffers`, `acceptOffer`, `declineOffer`, `markReady`, `getRiderOrder`. The rider pass: offer expiry, moving the hold to the next free scooter, `NO_SCOOTER` and its refund, the packing limit, and `seq` when the minimum wait ends. The customer's `rider_accepted` stage and rider first name. `endShift` with an offer. Migration 0019 and the `offer_timeout_s` setting.

**Not covered here:** GPS pings, the zone timer, `rider_moving`, handover, and live streams (part 2 and 3).
