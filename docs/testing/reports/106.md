**Deliberate breaks:** 19. 16 caught after two tests were strengthened and one invalid break was redone. Not caught alone, each stopped by a second layer: two suggestions for one scooter and two open requests (the database's one-per-scooter rules), and a stock check on a scooter off shift (the per-scooter step reads only open shifts).

**An existing test changed:** `TestOneBadEventNeverBlocksTheQueue` asserted the stand-in answer for restock events (`not_available_yet`, retryable), which was documented as lasting until v0.7.0. Its restock line now expects the real answer (`invalid_event` for an empty payload). Its check that a retryable rejection is not kept moved to the new `TestARetryableRejectionIsNotKept`, with a scan when no shift is open. The other assertions are unchanged.
