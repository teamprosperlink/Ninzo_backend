## 1. Scope
- **`internal/sim.Run(router, scenario)`** draws gates at random in a box, snapped to the nearest road (redrawn when more than 60 m from one). For each order it applies two rules:
  - `today`: the distance rule of `availability.PickScooter`, the nearest rider by straight line within `min(max_search_radius_m, straight_line_speed_kmh × sweet_spot_budget_s)`. It does not model stock, basket or zone checks.
  - `road`: the fastest rider by road within `sweet_spot_budget_s`, from one `Table` call.
- **`Timed`** uses the picked rider's OSRM route line to find, to about a metre, where it first comes within `zone_radius_m` (the timer starts) and then within `arrival_perimeter_m` (it stops). Each segment is sampled, so a dip inside between its ends is found too. Then one OSRM route through rider, start, stop and gate (`routing.Via`, with the line's heading at start and stop and `continue_straight`) gives the wait and the timed leg as its legs. Signal and turn costs count, and the path is the one ridden.
  - A pick with no road, or a route that ends further than the perimeter from the gate, is **stuck**: served, never in time, and counted as an endless leg in the percentiles.
  - Whether the rider starts inside the zone or the perimeter is judged at the route's start (the snapped rider).
- **Measures:** served, refused and stuck; in time of served and **in time of all orders placed**; the timed leg and wait at p50 and p90; and `sim.Wilson`, the 95% interval for a share.
- **`cmd/sim -seeds K`** pools K seeds and prints the shares with 95% intervals.
- **`routing.Via` and `routing.Bearing`** are new in the routing client, with the same fallback and breaker as `Route`.
- **Placeholders stop the run:** a straight-line placeholder answer anywhere ends it, because a simulation on straight lines proves nothing about roads.

## 3. Deliberate breaks
Each break was made alone, the tests run, then the code restored (checked with `cmp`).

| Break | Caught by |
|---|---|
| The timer starts at the perimeter, not the zone edge | `... TimedS:0 ..., want wait 130 s` |
| Today ignores its straight-line reach | `today took a rider 500 m away, past its 333 m reach`. It was not caught at first; that case was added, then the break re-run |
| A stuck ride counted as in time | `a route ending 300 m short: {... InTime:true ...}` |
| A pick with no road counted as refused | `{Refused:true ...}` |
| In time per order divided by served | `... InTimePerOrder:0 ...` |
| Stuck rides left out of the percentiles | `... TimedP90 ...` not endless |

## 4. Independent review
- **First pass: FAIL.** The code was sound enough, but the claims overstated the data. All findings are fixed:
  1. **The in-time share hid volume.** Today gets about 2.8× as many customers in time per order placed. w8, the CHANGELOG and project_status now lead with in time per order and state the product trade-off; the G24 advice is withdrawn.
  2. **The road rule's high share follows from the rule itself.** w8 says so, and the golden test no longer treats it as proof.
  3. **Statistics.** There are now 5 seeds with pooled counts and 95% Wilson intervals, and the precision quoted is reduced.
  4. **The timed leg was re-routed.** It is now one route through the points, with headings, as legs. Each segment is sampled, not just at its midpoint. Inside checks use the route's start.
  5. **Unreachable picks counted as refused,** which flattered today's rule. They are now stuck (failures), and stuck rides count as endless legs in the percentiles.
  6. **The `PickScooter` claim.** It is narrowed to "its distance rule only".
  7. **CI cost.** The golden is down to 40 orders and one run (5.5 s).
- **Second pass:** PASS; it reproduced the w8 numbers. Its minor point is fixed: a repeated point in a route line no longer gives a false heading of north (`headingAt`, tested).

## 5. Known limits
- Riders are free for every order (no fleet state), so served counts are an upper bound.
- No placement (W4) or demand (W7).
- One fixed layout of six riders per zone.
- The profile's numbers are placeholders until the road test. The comparison shows the shape of the trade-off, not budgets.
