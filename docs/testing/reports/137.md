# Test report: #137, menu and prices, gates, hubs and hub staff in the console (v0.9.0 part E)

Date: 2026-11-11. Local Postgres only. Migration 0036 is not on Supabase.

## What was built
- Menu: `listAdminMenu`, `createMenuItem` (added hidden), `updateMenuItem` (each price change keeps its reason in the price history; orders keep the price they were placed at, in `order_items.unit_paise`), `getPriceHistory` (newest first).
- Gates: `listAdminGates` (newest first, by zone, paged), `updateGate` (a new version with who and why; a moved pin must stay inside the gate's zone; text cleaned as for a customer's own gate; a gate whose customer was erased cannot be changed), `getGateHistory`.
- Hubs and staff: `listAdminHubs`, `createHub`, `updateHub` (a moved pin stays in its zone), `listHubStaff`, `addHubStaff`, `updateHubStaff`. Suspending or moving staff to another hub ends their sessions. A hub_manager sees and changes only their own hub.
- Earning rates are settings (`pay_per_order_paise`, `incentive_*`, `relocation_paise_per_minute`, `relocation_max_paid_s`), entered through part D's settings approvals, so this part adds nothing for them.
- Migration 0036: `gate_history.changed_by`; the gate history guard now also keeps it; `price_history.created_at` takes the real time (`clock_timestamp()`), so a price change that waited on another sorts after it.
