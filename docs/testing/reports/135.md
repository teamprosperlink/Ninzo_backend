# Test report: #135, rider review in the console (v0.9.0 part C)

Date: 2026-11-11. Local Postgres only. No migration.

## What was built
- `listRiderReviews`: applicants, and active riders whose latest verification failed or expired (including one past its expiry), oldest first, paged. Only the last four phone digits are shown. Suspended riders are not listed.
- `decideRider`: approve (verified, with an active zone_id), suspend (also how an applicant is turned down), reinstate (suspended only, verified, active zone). Every decision is audited with before and after.
- Suspending ends the rider's sessions through the session lock, then their shift through `shifts.EndShiftTx`, now the one way a shift ends for both the rider and the console. An order only offered goes back to be offered again, and its history names the console user. A rider carrying an accepted order cannot be suspended: 409 `order_in_progress` (decision on #135).

## Deliberate breaks (14, each reverted)
All caught: approving an unverified rider; approving twice; approving with no zone_id; a paused zone accepted; the shift kept on suspension; sessions kept; mid-order suspension as a 500; the wrong actor in order history; suspended riders listed; expiry ignored; the full phone shown; the wrong order; no permission check; the wrong audit action. Two were first missed (zone_id and the audit action) and the tests were tightened until they were caught.

## Independent review
2 FAILs, both fixed: a mid-order suspension answered 503, now 409 `order_in_progress`; a deadlock between a suspension and the rider ending their shift, fixed by taking the rider's session lock first and `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` on the rider row. Notes taken: the order history actor, active zones only, expired verifications, suspended riders out of the queue, the same latest-verification rule as shift start, and tests for each. Left as it was: the matcher can still offer to a rider suspended a moment ago; the offer cannot be accepted and expires, as after any shift end.
