## 1. Scope
- **`internal/placement`** is pure.
  - `Greedy(coverage, weights, n, q, capacity)`: maximum expected covering, maximising Σ w_d (1 − q^{k_d}). It adds one scooter at a time where the gain w_d q^{k_d} (1 − q) summed over covered d is largest. Ties go to the lower index, and it respects capacity. It returns the expected share, the covered share and each pick's gain.
  - `Matrix(router, candidates, demand, budget, speed)`: coverage within the budget by road, one `Table` call per demand point. Unreachable never covers, and a placeholder answer is an error.
- **`sim.Place`:** candidates are a grid snapped to roads (dropped when more than 60 m from one). Demand is gates drawn with a seed of its own, and the budget is `sweet_spot_budget_s`.
- **`cmd/sim -place`** (in fleet mode, with `-q`): places on seed 1000 + seed and evaluates on seed, seed + 1, and so on.
- **CI:** the `routes` job runs the placement golden.

  - The overlap counts were wrong: road 6 of 8 rows did not overlap, not 7; today's overlapped in 5 of 8.
  - The objective favours the road rule and was not disclosed.
  - The fixed baseline is hand-picked and weak.
  - A failing fit was not retried, and negative weights were accepted.
  - The golden's `Expected > Covered` check could never fail.
- **Fixed:**
  - Re-run as four independent fits with per-fit paired differences, and w4 rewritten on them.
  - The bias and the weak baseline are disclosed.
  - `Matrix` asks again after a straight-line answer, up to three attempts in all.
  - Weights must be finite and not negative.
  - The golden checks 0 < Expected < Covered and Covered ≥ 0.05.
  - The failing fit turned out to be a routing bug, fixed in #217.
- **Second pass: FAIL for wording, PASS for the numbers.** All 12 pooled rows and 32 per-fit values match. Fixed:

## 5. Known limits
- The placed points are road-snapped grid points, not legal waiting spots.
- Demand is uniform.
- q = 0.2 is a placeholder (measured busy shares were 2 to 30%).
- One placement per run, with no time bands and no relocation.
