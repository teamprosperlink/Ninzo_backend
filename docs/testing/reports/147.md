# Test report: console support actions, the server side (#147)

Date: 2026-11-12. Local Postgres only. Migration 0042 is not on Supabase. Independent review: not yet done (to be run before the PR).

## What was built
- `internal/console/support.go`, mounted by `MountSupport` in `apps/console-api/main.go`: `listTickets`, `getTicket`, `replyTicket`, `closeTicket` (permission `tickets.manage`), `requestRefund` (`refunds.request`), `issueReplacement` and `issueCredit` (`remedies.issue`). Every write needs an Idempotency-Key, is audited with `audit.Record`, and saves its reply in the same transaction (`changeWith`, which differs from `change` only in letting the handler pick 200 or 202).
- Refunds: `requestRefund` locks the order, then its paid payment (the order the automatic refunds also take them in), reads what was refunded (all non-failed refunds) and the waiting refund approval, and refuses with `refund_too_large` when the amount is more than paid less both. Then, with `refund_approval_paise` read for the order's zone (20000 when no row holds it): if the console's refunds on the order, plus the waiting approval, plus this amount are at or below it, the refund is made at once (200); otherwise `RequestChange` creates an approval of kind `refund` (202; a second one for the order is `approval_pending`). `console.ApplyRefund`, registered as the `refund` kind, makes the refund when a second admin approves, in the name and with the reason of the person who asked, if it still fits (else the approval fails with `refund_too_large` or `not_paid`). A rejected, cancelled or lapsed approval no longer counts as pending, so its amount is free again.
- The refund itself is `orders.RecordSupportRefund` (new, `internal/orders/support_refund.go`): a row of the existing `refunds` table, `processed` at once for the test provider and `pending` for Razorpay (the starting status now comes from one function, `refundStatus`, which `refund()` also uses). Its `reason_code` is `support_refund_<n>` because `(payment_id, reason_code)` is unique; the order's `seq` moves so the customer's views refresh.
- Replacements and credits are rows of the existing `remedies` table (one of each type per order, so a second is `remedy_exists`). A replacement checks every item is on the order and not more than ordered (`item_not_on_order`).
- Tickets: the list is newest first with `status` and `type` filters and the console cursor; `subject` is the first 80 characters of the customer's first message. A reply is a `ticket_messages` row with `sender = 'support'` (which the customer's `getTicket` already reads) plus one `push.customer` outbox event `{customer_id, ticket_id, stage: "ticket_reply"}`. A reply to, or a second close of, a resolved ticket is 409 `ticket_closed`.
- Migration 0042: `zone_settings.refund_approval_paise`; `refunds.requested_by` and `note`; `remedies.created_by` and `items`; `support_tickets.closed_at`, `closed_by`, `close_reason` (and an index); `ticket_messages.author_id`. The setting is one line in `internal/geo/settings.go`.
- The order timeline (`internal/console/orders.go`) now fills `refunds[].requested_by`, `remedies[].created_by` and `items`, names the person on their events, and uses `closed_at` for `ticket.resolved`.
- Tests that exist on main were not edited. `console_test.go` gained the two fixture lines that mount `MountSupport` and the `refund` kind, as #146 did for `MountOrders`. No exact field list in `tests/contracts` grew.

## Deliberate breaks (19 compiled and ran, each reverted, all caught)
1 limit `>` changed to `>=`; 2 earlier console refunds left out of the cumulative total; 3 waiting approvals left out of "too large"; 4 refunds already made left out of "too large"; 5 both row locks taken out of the refund; 6 the apply step skips its "still fits" check; 7 the approved refund is made in the approver's name; 8 a reply to a resolved ticket is allowed; 9 the reply text is put into the push; 10 the refund route needs `remedies.issue`; 11 a replacement may exceed the quantity ordered; 12 `requested_by` hidden in the timeline; 13 a test-provider refund starts pending; 14 rejected approvals still count as waiting; 15 a console remedy's reason put back into the timeline events; 16 credit amount upper bound removed; 17 expired approvals not lapsed before counting; 18 close ignores the reason given; 19 a reply forgets its author.

Three things the breaks found, all fixed in the tests and not in the code:
- Breaks 2 and 4 first left a variable unused and did not compile; they were redone so they compile. Break 16 first matched nothing; it was redone.
- Break 5 (no row locks) was not caught at first: the requests of the first concurrent test were all from one person, and the session check (`identity.CheckSession` locks the actor) already made one person's calls take turns. The test now uses eight people and thirty requests. With the locks taken out it fails (22 of 30 answered 500, because the table's own trigger, `refunds_within_payment`, refuses the overshoot; the paid amount holds, but the answer is an error); with the locks it passes.

## Independent review
Fixed after the review:
1. Automatic refunds after a console refund. `refundPaid` (customer cancel, `failOrder`, the NO_SCOOTER sweep) and `refundMissingFood` (handover) now insert at most the payment less its non-failed refunds (`refundLeft`, payment row locked), insert nothing when nothing is left, and cap the owed `refund` remedy the same way (no remedy when nothing is left, as the amount must be above zero). Four tests in `internal/orders/support_refund_test.go` drive the real paths (cancel, a rider's accident, handover with missing food, the NO_SCOOTER timeout): each succeeds and the total never passes the payment.
2. `getRefund` keeps the newest refund's reason, method, status and `expected_by`, with `amount` the total refunded so far. The contract's `Refund` has one `amount` (Money) with no wording tying it to one refund, so no contract change. Tested with two refunds (1000 + 3900 shows 4900) and with a console refund then a cancel.
3. `lapse()` in `requestRefund` runs before the order is locked; the waiting-approval sum also ignores expired rows. `RequestChange` still calls `lapse` on the 202 path; it finds nothing to update in normal use.
4. Migration 0042 starts with one `LOCK TABLE zone_settings, refunds, remedies, support_tickets, ticket_messages IN ACCESS EXCLUSIVE MODE`, as 0024 does.
5. `known_downsides.md`: three rows added (ticket subject, disabled requester, support cannot cancel its approval); the row saying a console refund can break the customer's cancel was removed.

Deliberate breaks (each compiled, was caught, was reverted): (a) `refundPaid` inserts the full amount: 3 tests fail (cancel, failed order, NO_SCOOTER); (b) the missing-food cap removed: the handover test fails; (c) `getRefund` shows only the newest amount: 2 tests fail.
