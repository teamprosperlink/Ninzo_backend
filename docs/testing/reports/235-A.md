## Secret scan
The CI `secrets` job (gitleaks, #238) flagged the new Bruno files under its generic-api-key rule. Line 16 is `Idempotency-Key: bruno-<operation>-0001`, a fixed request label, not a secret. `.gitleaks.toml` keeps every default rule and allows only the regex `Idempotency-Key: bruno-[A-Za-z]+-[0-9]{4}`; every Idempotency-Key in contracts/bruno matches it (0 that do not).
