## 1. Scope
- **`sim.RunFleet(router, FleetScenario)`:** orders arrive as a Poisson stream (`OrdersPerHour`, `Hours`). For each rule, on the same arrivals and gates, riders start free at their nodes. At each arrival only free riders are candidates, and the rule picks as in v1. The chosen rider is busy for `PackS`, then the ride to the gate (OSRM), then `HandoverS`, then the ride back (OSRM). A stuck ride still costs a ride there and back by the straight line at the slow speed.
- **Results:** `FleetResult` adds `RefusedBusy` (the rule would have taken a rider had they been free), `RefusedReach` (nobody within the rule's reach, free or not) and each rider's `BusyShare`. One Table call per order over every rider's node gives both.
- **`drawGates`** is taken out of `Run` unchanged. v1's golden numbers are unchanged (16 and 4 served).
- **`cmd/sim -fleet N -orders-per-hour R -hours H -pack P -handover S`** pools seeds and gives 95% intervals. The fleet golden runs in CI's `routes` job.

## 4. Independent review
- **First pass: PASS for the code, FAIL for the w8 wording.** Both claims are fixed:
  1. **"Almost every refusal is reach" was wrong as counted.** "Busy" counted only a whole-fleet-busy moment, so a rider in reach who was busy while free riders were out of reach showed as reach. The split is now by what the rule would have done had everyone been free, and the sweep is re-run. Reach is the larger cause (78 to 100% for today's rule), and busy grows with demand (up to 75 of 493).
  2. **"Placing riders better helps more" was not measured.** It is now stated as the hypothesis for W4.
- **Also taken:** tests for a rider free at exactly the arrival time and for a stuck ride's cost, and a note that a ride past the end counts in full (under about 3 points high).
- **Second pass:** PASS. It checked every w8 row against the sweep (the 78–100% claim holds: lowest 263/338 = 77.8%). Its two wording fixes are made: the gain from 4 to 6 riders is split by rule (2–9 points today, 1–4 road), and the golden line is updated.
