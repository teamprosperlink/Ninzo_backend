# Test report: #138, the audit log, arrival reviews and support alerts in the console (v0.9.0 part F)

Date: 2026-11-11. Local Postgres only. Migration 0037 is not on Supabase.

## What was built
- `listAuditEvents`: newest first, by time, actor, action and entity type, paged by row id. Secrets are hidden by key name, however deep: passwords, TOTP and other secrets, full phone numbers, tokens, Google subject ids and emails. `phone_last4` and harmless keys such as `otp_released_at` stay.
- `listArrivalReviews`: open ones first (soonest due), then the rest, newest first, paged.
- `decideArrivalReview`: `arrived_in_time` stops the timer at the rider's evidence, clears the miss and takes back a credit still owed; a credit already given is refused (`credit_applied`). `late` keeps the miss, and the background pass pays its credit. Each decision is in the order's history and the audit log. Migration 0037 records who decided and why.
- `listSupportAlerts`: what the system raised for a person, newest first. A refused handover's alert now names its order, and a lost signal now raises an alert (its problem report already said support was alerted).
- A timer stopped by a review stays stopped: the missed-timer pass and a later arrival respect it.

## Deliberate breaks (22, each reverted)
All caught: no scrubbing, at the top or nested; `phone_last4` hidden; `otp_released_at` over-hidden; a Google subject shown; the audit log in the wrong order; the actor filter or the from filter ignored; no permission check; `arrived_in_time` keeping the miss; the wrong stop time; deciding twice; deciding past the due time; an owed credit kept; an applied credit ignored; no order event; open reviews not first across pages; alerts of any topic; the miss pass undoing a stopped timer; an arrival undoing it; no lost-signal alert; a handover alert with no order. One first failed to compile and was redone as a valid change.
