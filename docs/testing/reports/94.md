# Test report: remedies, late-arrival review and the longer ETA (#94)

- Date: 2026-11-09

## Scope
v0.6.0 part A. Credits for a missed timer, arrival reviews, the missing-food refund, `acceptLongerEta` with `eta_offer`, `payment_status` for part refunds, and `order_state` on the rider stream for order changes. Migration 0021.

**Not covered here:** applying a credit to a later order, deciding reviews (console), making ETA offers (needs measured times), rider problems and reassignment (part B).

## Scenarios
| Rule | Tested |
|---|---|
| A missed timer with fresh pings owes one credit of the console's amount, raising `seq` once; the order carries on to handover | Yes |
| With no amount set, the credit has a null amount | Yes |
| While the signal is lost, the credit waits; after 10 minutes past the timer it goes ahead; the signal returning releases it | Yes |
| A late ping from inside the timer at the gate opens a review that holds the credit, until it times out after 10 minutes; a ping from before the timer opens nothing; a review decided "in time" means no credit | Yes |
| A partial handover refunds the missing food as `missing_food`, shows a refund remedy, and leaves `payment_status` paid; a full handover owes nothing | Yes |
| A longer ETA: shown at `no_scooter`, accepted with consent, stock held again, the agreed time replaces `wait_deadline`, the search starts again; a repeat answers the same; wrong, expired, no-stock and another customer's offers are refused | Yes |
| The rider stream sends `order_state` for Ready and arrival | Yes |

**Deliberate breaks:** 20. 19 caught after two tests were tightened and six invalid breaks were redone. Removing the once-only rule for a credit was not caught alone: the pass never picks an order that already has a credit.
