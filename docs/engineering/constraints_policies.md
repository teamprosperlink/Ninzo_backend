# Constraints and policies: ninzo-backend

Version: 0.0.1 · Updated: 2026-11-04

## 1. Constraints (things we cannot change)
Budget, deadlines, regulations, platform limits, team size, external dependencies.

## 2. Engineering policies (things we chose to enforce)
- Architecture boundaries and how they are enforced.
- Dependency policy.
- Data and privacy policy.
- Security policy (secrets, auth, logging of personal data).
- Test policy: what must be tested; no editing of existing tests to pass.
- Release policy: versioning, tags, rollback.

## 3. Exceptions
Each exception: what, why, who approved, expiry date.
